Solar PV Array

Fixed-Tilt Generation

South-facing fixed-tilt arrays feed combiner boxes and run grid-tied with storage — generating by day, banking energy off-peak.

固定式支架陣列面朝正南，深藍色模組串接後匯入匯流箱，與儲能系統併網運轉，白天發電、離峰儲存。

Gantry lines drop through insulator strings into two main transformers, split by a firewall, stepping site power up for export.

門型架引接線經礙子串落至兩座主變壓器，中間防火牆隔離，將場站電力升壓送出。
